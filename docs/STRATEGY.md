# Inaya: long-term strategy

How Inaya grows from a new account into a character that earns. `docs/PLAN.md` covers
how content is produced; this covers why, in what order, and how it pays.

## 1. Positioning

**Karachi's car girl: a fashion eye, a car heart, and honest about car care.**

Why this can work:
- **Detailing studios and PPF installers need content.** Most of them post the same
  before-and-after photos. Inaya gives them polished, character-led content they can't
  easily make themselves.
- **Few women are visible in Pakistani car culture.** "Girls belong in car culture" is a
  story people want to share, and it sets her apart from every other car page.
- **An AI character can post daily at high quality and low cost.** Once the reference set
  is locked, the kit makes production a matter of hours, not shoot days.
- **The niche is specific.** Brands in it (PPF, coatings, tint, car care, tyres,
  accessories) have money to spend and a clear audience to reach.

## 2. The story

Real influencers grow because people follow a life, not a feed. Inaya's life runs on
**seasons**: about 12-week arcs with a goal, weekly episodes and a finale. The canon is in
`.claude/skills/inaya-brand-voice/SKILL.md`.

### Season 1: "Bijli Diaries" (weeks 1–12)

She brings her father's white 1990s coupe, Bijli, back to life. Every episode is a slot
for a partner studio or brand.

| Week | Episode | Partner slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The reveal: the cover comes off. "Isko wapas zinda karna hai." | None, pure story |
| 2 | Assessment at the studio: swirls under the inspection light | Detailing studio |
| 3 | Wash and decontamination | Car-care products |
| 4 | Paint correction | Detailing studio |
| 5 | The vote: gloss or matte PPF? The audience chooses | PPF brand |
| 6 | Install day with Ustad ji | PPF installer |
| 7 | Demo week: water beading, self-healing | PPF brand |
| 8 | Interior refresh | Interior or upholstery studio |
| 9 | Wheels and tint | Wheels, tyres, tint |
| 10 | First drive: coastal highway at golden hour | Fuel or auto app |
| 11 | Debut at a car meet | Event or community |
| 12 | Finale photoshoot and a tease for Season 2 | Season sponsor |

The week 5 vote is the engagement engine: the audience steers the story, so they come
back to see the result.

### Next seasons (options)

- **Season 2, "North ka safar":** a road trip up the Karakoram with Bijli. Partners:

- **Season 3, "Auto show season":** stands, launches, new cars. Partners: dealers,

## 3. Growth stages and money

Each stage has a gate. Move on when the numbers say so, not the calendar.

| Stage | Followers (guide) | Focus | Revenue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Launch | 0–5k | Lock her identity, post daily, finish Season 1 episodes 1–4 | None yet. Barter with 1–2 studios: their location and products in exchange for content they can repost |
| 2 · Local partners | 5k–25k | Studio Visit series, the first paid features | Paid studio features, content packages, booking-code commissions |
| 3 · Brands | 25k–100k | Season sponsorships, brand series | Brand campaigns, season sponsors, affiliate sales where available |
| 4 · Own products and IP | 100k+ | Products, licensing, a second character | Co-branded car-care kit, merch, platform payouts where eligible, licensing |

### Start on day one: the studio content service

The quickest money doesn't wait for followers. **Sell monthly content packages to
detailing and PPF studios:** for example, 12 images and 4 Reels of Inaya at their studio,
with their real products. The kit's R5 and BG1 prompts already do this. The studio posts
it on its own account, so the value doesn't depend on Inaya's follower count, and every
studio post shows her to a new audience.

The same system can later build characters for other businesses, as an agency service.

### Pricing: how to set it, not made-up numbers

- Benchmark against local micro-influencers and the cost of a studio's own photo shoot.
  Talk to 3–5 studios before setting rates.
- Sell **packages** (a monthly retainer), not single posts. Include usage rights: how
  long the partner may repost, and where.
- Start low for the first 2–3 partners in exchange for permission to use the results
  as case studies. Raise rates at each stage gate.
- Send every partner a short monthly report: reach, saves, profile visits, link
  clicks, code uses.

### Media kit (build at stage 2)

One page covering:
- Who Inaya is, and the audience (age, city, gender split).
- Average reach and engagement.
- Formats offered.
- Two or three case studies.
- How partnerships work, including the AI disclosure.

## 4. What to measure

| Stage | Watch |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Posting consistency, the QA pass rate, saves and shares per post, follower growth per week |
| 2 | Studio DMs and leads, partner repost reach, code uses |
| 3 | Reach per sponsored post vs organic, sponsor renewals |
| 4 | Product sales, licensing deals, revenue per post |

Saves and shares matter more than likes: they show that content was useful, which is
what partners pay for.

## 5. What the system needs to support this

| Need | Kit feature (build stage) |

| A believable life | Day planner with season arcs, continuity and the calendar (stage 4) |

| Proof for partners | Posting log with results, monthly partner report (stage 5) |
| A professional pitch | Media-kit page (stage 5+) |
| Scale | Automated image generation with your own API key (optional, stage 6) |

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Platforms limit or label AI content | Disclose openly, use the labels, avoid realistic deception |
| Backlash ("she's not real") | Own it in the bio and in content. The story and the usefulness are the point |
| Brands are wary of AI | Lead with studios (case studies), report results, offer exclusivity per category |
| Relying on one platform | Instagram first, then TikTok and YouTube Shorts; a WhatsApp channel or email list for owned reach |
| Legal and ethical | Paid-partnership labels, attributed claims, written permission for places, products and people; check local advertising rules |
| Identity drift at volume | The QA gate in `docs/PLAN.md`; never skip the reference images |
