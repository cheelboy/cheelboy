Workflow

  1. Master portrait (Ref A). Run the master prompt 6–10 times with no image attached. Keep the one face you'd use for years.
  2. Reference set. Attach Ref A and make the face turnaround (Ref B), full-body proportion sheet (Ref C) and expression sheet (Ref D).
  3. Scenes. Attach Ref A, B and C, in that order, to every scene. Ref A always stays Image 1.
  4. Video frames. Generate anchor frames for your lip-sync or image-to-video tool. Every ratio here is one the app offers: 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4 or 9:16.

Phase 1 · Master portrait

Plain backdrop, plain white tee, face fully visible. Re-roll rather than edit at this stage.

Phase 2 · Reference set

Attach only Ref A to B, C and D. They don't describe her face; Ref A does. Ref C adds her body description, because Ref A only shows her chest up. Save the keepers as A, B, C, D, plus one full body per outfit you'll reuse.

Phase 3 · Scenes

Attach Ref A, Ref B and Ref C. These prompts never describe her: the images carry her look, and text would compete with them. Add an outfit reference when the same outfit must match across shots.

Phase 4 · Video anchor frames

Frontal face, eyes on the lens, mouth relaxed, nothing covering the face, even light on both sides.

Fix prompts

Use on a generated image to pull it back on model. Keep Ref A attached as Image 1.

Modules

The building blocks, for writing your own scenes. The DNA is only for the master portrait. Once images are attached, describe outfit, scene, shot and light only.
