Workflow

  1. Master portrait (Ref A). Run the master prompt 6–10 times with no image attached. Keep the one face you'd use for years: clear skin, no mole. If you already have a Ref A with the old beauty mark, clean it with fix F7 instead.
  2. Reference set. Attach Ref A and make the face turnaround (Ref B), full-body proportion sheet (Ref C) and expression sheet (Ref D). All of them use plain white clothes, so you judge her face and body rather than an outfit.
  3. Shot builder. Pick a format (scene, selfie, product review, talking head), then place, car, outfit, action and camera. It writes the prompt and numbers the images to attach. Ref A, B and C always come first, in that order.
  4. Scenes. Ready-made everyday scenes: cafés, her car, the seafront, food streets, plus a studio-gig arrival and car events. The prompts tell the model to ignore the white reference clothes; if they leak through anyway, use fix F6.
  5. Studio gigs. Shots for a paid studio visit, plus prompts that put her into a photo of the real studio or a real product in her hands.
  6. Video frames. Generate anchor frames for your lip-sync or image-to-video tool. Every ratio here is one the app offers: 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4 or 9:16.

Nano Banana Pro rules

What every prompt here follows. Use the same rules when you write your own.

  1. Sentences, not tags. Purpose, then subject, outfit, action, place, camera, light and style, ending with the aspect ratio.
  2. Give every image a job. Image 1 is always her face. Say what to take from each image, and what to ignore.
  3. Don't describe her face with images attached. The image is the description, and text competes with it.
  4. Say what you want. "Clear skin", "an unbadged car", then one short "No …" sentence for hard limits.
  5. Size up for sheets. The pill shows the resolution to use where your tool lets you pick it (AI Studio, the API, Flow).
  6. One change per edit. Say what stays the same before what changes.

Phase 1 · Master portrait

Plain backdrop, plain white tee, face fully visible, clear skin with no mole. Re-roll rather than edit at this stage.

Phase 2 · Reference set

Attach only Ref A to B, C and D. Every reference is shot in plain white clothes: a white tee for the face sheets, and a fitted white tee, white leggings and bare feet on a mid-grey backdrop for the body sheet, so her proportions read without an outfit in the way. The prompts don't describe her face; Ref A does. Ref C adds her body description, because Ref A only shows her chest up. Save the keepers as A, B, C, D, plus one full body per outfit you'll reuse. Ref E (a large front view) and Ref H (hands and jewelry) are optional, but Ref H pays off in product shots where her hands are close to the lens.
